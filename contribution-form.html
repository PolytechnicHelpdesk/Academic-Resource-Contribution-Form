Welcome to the Polytechnic Helpdesk — Academic Resource Contribution Form

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Polytechnic Helpdesk.

Your contribution plays an important role in helping Polytechnic students across Assam access quality academic resources and prepare effectively for their examinations.

Through this form, you can submit:

  • Study Materials
  • Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs)
  • Notes
  • Lab Manuals
  • Practical Files
  • Assignments
  • E-Books
  • Other academic resources

Before you submit, please ensure that:

  1. The uploaded material is relevant to Polytechnic education.
  2. The files are clear, complete, and in readable condition.
  3. You have the right or permission to share the material.
  4. You do not upload copyrighted or confidential content without authorization.
Please note: All submissions will be reviewed before publication. Polytechnic Helpdesk reserves the right to accept, edit, organize, or reject any submission that does not meet our guidelines.

Thank you for helping us build a reliable and comprehensive learning resource for Polytechnic students. Your support is sincerely appreciated.
